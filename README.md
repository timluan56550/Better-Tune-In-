# Better Tune In
A small Phasmaphobia like game where you operate an old radio desk to investigate radio frequencies. You can tune through frequencies to identify evidence. Survive your shift from 10 PM to 6 AM and Try to select the correct anomaly.

## How to play
- You have 4 items on your desk: A radio, A crank, A notebook, and some Coffee!

- Turn on the radio by clicking the red rectangle. Then above you can switch through different frequencies.

- Most frequencies will have static, but ever so often a creepy noise will go through! Mark this in your Notebook

- Keeping the radio on costs battery, to get more, hold down on the crank to slowly generate more energy. Keep in mind that you can't hold it for too long at a time as it might shock you. A message will popup telling you when to release.

-Marking evidence is pretty easy, just find the range the frequency is in (column),  and what type it is (row)

- After getting enough evidence, head over to the guess tab. This is where you make your selection, if you have sufficient evidence you may only have one option left. If you still have options, find more evidence! Otherwise if you don't have any option, go back and see what was wrong.

- Your time limit is from 10 PM to 6 AM, when it reaches 6 AM, your radio will turn off and now you have to choose an anomaly!

- The coffee will heal you 40 hp one time.

- After clicking submit and confirming, you will get a file report and you are able to see if you guessed right or not
